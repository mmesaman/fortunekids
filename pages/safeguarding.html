Child Safeguarding

Last updated: January 2026

Our commitment

The safety and well-being of every child is Fortune Kids’ absolute priority. Everyone who is part of the organization —staff, volunteers and partners— shares the responsibility of protecting children.

Core principles

  • Best interests of the child: every decision starts from the welfare of minors.
  • Zero tolerance: any sign of risk triggers immediate action under our internal protocol.
  • Ongoing training: the whole team receives regular training in child protection.
  • Dignity in communication: we never publish images or data identifying a minor without the informed consent of their guardians.

Incident reporting

If you notice any situation that could compromise the safety of a minor within our programs, report it immediately to info@fortunekids.org. All reports are handled with the strictest confidentiality.
