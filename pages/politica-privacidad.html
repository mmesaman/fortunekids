Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Data controller

Fortune Kids, a local organization based in Arusha, Tanzania, is the controller of the personal data collected through this website and its contact channels.

2. Data we collect

  • Data you provide voluntarily through the contact form: name, email and message.
  • Browsing data collected anonymously, for statistical purposes, if you enable analytical cookies.

3. Purpose

We use your data exclusively to answer your enquiries, manage your involvement as a donor or volunteer, and keep you informed about our activities if you request it.

4. Retention and your rights

We keep your data only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose. You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection at any time by writing to info@fortunekids.org.

5. Child protection

We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Images and names of children featured in our communications are always published in accordance with our child protection policy and with the consent of their guardians.
