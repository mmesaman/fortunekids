About us

A local organization with deep roots in Arusha and one conviction: the future is built through education.

Our mission & vision

Mission

Educate, protect and empower

We support children, young people and families in Arusha through education, health and vocational training, so that every person can build their own path with dignity.

Vision

Communities that no longer need help

We aspire to an Arusha where childhood is protected, young people have real opportunities and communities are self-reliant.
