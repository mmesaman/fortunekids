Why child sponsorship matters

Your sponsorship helps break the cycle of poverty by giving a child consistent support and the chance to thrive.

Education

School fees, uniforms, books and learning materials.

Health

Medical checkups, treatment and hygiene support.

Nutrition

Daily meals and nutritional care.

Protection

Safe spaces and child welfare support.

How the program works

Step 1

Choose to sponsor

Pick a child from this page and send us an email.

Step 2

Get matched

We will connect you to learn about the child’s dreams.

Step 3

Receive updates

School reports, stories and photos showing your impact.

Step 4

Change a future

Your support creates lasting opportunities for education and growth.
