What we do

Three complementary programs supporting every child from school to adult life.

Education & child protection

We work so that no child in the communities we partner with is left out of the classroom. We cover school fees and supplies, offer tutoring and after-school support, and train families and institutions in child protection.

  • School scholarships and learning materials
  • Remedial classes and literacy
  • Child rights and protection workshops
  • Individualized family support

Health & well-being

A healthy child can learn, play and dream. We organize regular medical check-ups, hygiene and nutrition campaigns, and guarantee access to treatment when a child needs it.

  • Medical check-ups and pediatric follow-up
  • Nutrition education for families
  • Hygiene and prevention campaigns
  • Referral and support for medical treatment

Youth & training

The future of Arusha’s youth depends on having a trade, a job and a voice of their own. We offer vocational training, career guidance and micro-support to start their own projects.

  • Vocational training in local trades
  • Career guidance and job placement
  • Young entrepreneurship mentoring
  • Micro-loans and support for own initiatives

These programs are possible thanks to people like you

Every donation becomes scholarships, medical check-ups and real training for the youth of Arusha.
