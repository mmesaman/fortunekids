Stories that inspire

Education

Amina returns to school after a year out of the classroom

When her family lost its income, Amina left school at the age of 9. The Fortune Kids team found her case during a community visit and offered her a scholarship covering tuition, uniform and supplies. Today Amina is back in 4th grade and dreams of becoming a teacher so that no girl in her neighborhood is left out of school.

Health

The medical check-up campaign reaches 40 families

Working with local health professionals, we completed a new round of pediatric check-ups: weight, vaccinations, eyesight and nutrition. Five children were referred for treatment and followed up until discharge. Families received hands-on training on balanced meals using local products.

Youth

The program’s first young graduates open their own business

After completing the vocational training pathway, Joseph and Baraka opened a sewing workshop in central Arusha. With an initial micro-grant and ongoing mentoring, they already employ two more people from the community. Their workshop now serves as a training placement for the program’s new students.
